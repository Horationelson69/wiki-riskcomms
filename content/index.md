# Crisis Intelligence Wiki

\> **Stability architecture for the Polycrisis.**
\> A living knowledge base maintained by [RiskComms](https://riskcomms.com).

## What Is This Wiki?

The **Crisis Intelligence Wiki** is a compounding knowledge base for crisis communication professionals. It contains **case studies**, **response frameworks**, and **pattern analysis** drawn from 20+ years of crisis communication experience.

The wiki is updated weekly as new incidents emerge and patterns crystallize. All content is compiled from public sources, documented incidents, and field analysis.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Crisis Intelligence Wiki?

The Crisis Intelligence Wiki is a public knowledge base maintained by RiskComms that documents crisis communication case studies, frameworks, and cross-cutting patterns from real-world incidents.

### Who maintains this wiki?

This wiki is maintained by [Philippe Borremans](https://riskcomms.com/about). It draws from 10+ years of crisis communication experience and field analysis.

### How often is the wiki updated?

The wiki is updated weekly with new case studies as incidents emerge, and monthly with synthesized themes and framework refinements.

### Can I use the frameworks in this wiki?

The frameworks are provided for educational and informational purposes. For implementation support or tailored crisis, risk, or emergency communication strategies, [contact RiskComms directly](https://riskcomms.com/briefing).

### What types of crises are covered?

The wiki covers a wide range of crisis types, including infrastructure failures, product recalls, executive misconduct, data breaches, natural hazards, and social media-driven incidents.
